01 / Organization

About PHRINUSOMYIS™

General information about the organization.

What is PHRINUSOMYIS™?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization that develops, owns and administers sports competitions and ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ worldwide.

What does PHRINUSOMYIS™ do?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ develops and administers sports competitions, creates original sports formats and leads ZEAVIQ SPORTS™.

Where is PHRINUSOMYIS™ headquartered?

New York, USA, is the headquarters of PHRINUSOMYIS™.

Where can I find official information about PHRINUSOMYIS™?

The official homepage provides organizational information and direct access to the organization's active publication and information pages.

General enquiries and department-specific enquiries can be directed through the official Contact page.

02 / Sporting Identity

ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ & VIKQUATHEL™

Information about the organization's sporting identity.

What is ZEAVIQ SPORTS™?

ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ is part of the sporting identity and activities of PHRINUSOMYIS™, encompassing original sports formats and competitive experiences.

Is ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ part of PHRINUSOMYIS™?

Yes. ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ is part of PHRINUSOMYIS™ and its sporting activities.

What is VIKQUATHEL™?

VIKQUATHEL™ is a competition within the ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ sporting identity.

Official information and announcements are communicated through PHRINUSOMYIS™ channels.
