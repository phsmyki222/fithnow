About PHRINUSOMYIS™

General information about the organization.

What is PHRINUSOMYIS™?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization that develops, owns and administers sports competitions and Sports Theater™ worldwide.

The organization creates original sports formats that break away from traditional sports structures.

What does PHRINUSOMYIS™ do?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ develops and administers sports competitions, creates original sports formats and leads its Sports Theater™ concept.

Sports Theater™

Understanding the organization's Sports Theater™ concept.

What is Sports Theater™?

Sports Theater™ is a PHRINUSOMYIS™ sports concept built around original competitive formats and distinctive sporting experiences.

Is Sports Theater™ part of PHRINUSOMYIS™?

Yes. Sports Theater™ is part of the sports identity and activities of PHRINUSOMYIS™.
