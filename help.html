01
Organization

About PHRINUSOMYIS™

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization that develops, owns and administers sports competitions and ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ worldwide.
PHRINUSOMYIS™ develops and administers sports competitions, creates original sports formats and leads ZEAVIQ SPORTS™.
New York, USA is the headquarters of PHRINUSOMYIS™.
The official homepage provides organizational information and access to the active publication and information pages.
General and department enquiries can be directed through the official Contact page.
02
Sporting Identity

ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ & VIKQUATHEL™

ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ forms part of the sporting identity and activities of PHRINUSOMYIS™, encompassing original sports formats and competitive experiences.
Yes. ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ is part of PHRINUSOMYIS™ and its sporting activities.
VIKQUATHEL™ is a competition within the ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ sporting identity. Official information and announcements are communicated through PHRINUSOMYIS™ channels.
The homepage and Newswire provide organizational information and published competition announcements.
Use the official Contact page and identify the competition or participation subject of your enquiry.
03
Publications

Newswire & Magazine

The PHRINUSOMYIS™ Newswire is the publication channel for official announcements, news releases and organizational statements. Visit Newswire.
PHRINUSOMYIS™ Magazine presents sporting perspectives, editorial features, stories, photography and selected editorial coverage. Explore Magazine.
Newswire communicates official announcements, news releases and organizational statements. Magazine presents editorial features, photography, stories and selected coverage.
Official announcements are published through the PHRINUSOMYIS™ Newswire.
Press and media enquiries can be directed through the official Contact page.
04
Organizational Enquiries

Events, Partnerships & Employment

Confirmed event information is communicated through official announcements. Dates, locations and event details should be taken from published PHRINUSOMYIS™ communications.
Event-related enquiries can be submitted through the Contact page. Identify the event and the subject of your enquiry.
Organizations may submit partnership and cooperation enquiries through the Contact page. Include the organization name, proposed cooperation and relevant contact information.
Employment and recruitment enquiries may be directed through the Contact page. Include your professional background and area of work.
Include your name, organization where relevant, purpose of the enquiry, a concise description and any relevant supporting information.

Copyright and intellectual property information is available on the official Copyright page.
Accessibility information and related guidance are available on the official Accessibility page.
Identify the page, describe the issue and provide relevant details through the official Contact page.
