PHRINUSOMYIS™: The Next Chapter of Competitive Sport

The story of competition reaches across thousands of years. Its structures have changed, but the human desire to test ability, resolve and performance remains.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ Editorial Magazine Feature Official Publication

Competition is one of the oldest expressions of human ability. Across generations, people have tested strength, skill, endurance and resolve against one another.

The forms have changed throughout history. The settings, rules and expectations have evolved, but competition continues to place individuals and teams before a test in which performance matters and an outcome must be determined.

Modern sport developed established structures that brought organisation, consistency and international recognition to competition. Those structures became a defining part of the sporting world.

Travel Back to 3000 BC

To understand the long history of competition, the story reaches back thousands of years. In the ancient world, physical contests were connected to community life, preparation, strength and standing among others.

Ancient civilisations developed their own traditions of physical contests. The settings and practices differed, but competition offered a way to demonstrate ability and confront a test in front of others.

From ancient contests to modern international sport, the history of competition reflects a continuing evolution in how people organise, perform and measure sporting achievement.

Competition is not confined to one era. Its history continues wherever people create a contest and step forward to meet it. PHRINUSOMYIS™ Magazine

Returning to the Present

Today's sporting landscape includes established disciplines, international competitions and familiar governing structures. It also leaves room for original formats and different ways to organise a sporting contest.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization that develops, owns and administers sports competitions and Sports Theater™ worldwide. It creates original sports formats that break away from traditional sports structures.

A Different Direction

PHRINUSOMYIS™ develops original competitions with their own identity. Sports Theater™ is part of this sporting direction, providing the concept through which its sporting ideas are presented.

SOUL SURVIVE™ is a competition within the Sports Theater™ concept. Its themes include will, resolve, endurance, discipline and performance under pressure.

Different formats can create different demands on competitors. Each contest has its own purpose, structure and outcome, while retaining the central significance of performance.

The Next Chapter

The evolution of sport has been shaped over time by people who created contests, developed rules and introduced new ways to measure performance.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ develops and administers its own sporting competitions and Sports Theater™ worldwide. Its work forms part of the continuing story of competitive sport and original sporting formats.

The history of competition stretches back thousands of years. Its continuing story will be written through the formats people create, the competitors who enter them and the performances that follow.
