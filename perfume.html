THE SIGNIFICANCE OF FRAGRANCE

More Than a Scent.
An Expression of Identity.

Across generations and cultures, fragrance has occupied a place beyond the ordinary. It has been associated with personal expression, occasions, character and memory.

A fragrance can become part of a person's presence. Its impression may remain long after the moment itself has passed.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ approaches fragrance as part of its merchandise collection, presenting a distinct product identity through the release of Edition 001.

The edition carries a defined production allocation of 1,000 bottles, presented in two selected sizes: 50 ml and 100 ml.
