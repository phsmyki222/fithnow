The Story

Fragrance Is More Than a Scent

For generations, fragrance has been more than something people wear. It has been a personal signature — an expression of character, memory, presence and identity.

Across cultures and generations, a fragrance has carried the ability to leave an impression long after someone has departed.

A fragrance is personal. It becomes part of how someone is remembered.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ brings this enduring tradition into its merchandise collection with the release of its first fragrance edition.

Edition 001 carries a defined production allocation of 1,000 bottles, presented in two selected sizes. This first release establishes the opening chapter of the PHRINUSOMYIS™ fragrance collection.

Whether another edition follows will be determined by the direction of the collection and the response to this release.
