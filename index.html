The Organization

Sport Beyond Traditional Structures.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization with a distinct approach to competitive sport. It develops, owns and administers original competitions and sporting concepts across international markets.

Its work extends beyond conventional competition formats, bringing together original sporting ideas, structured contests and new expressions of competitive performance.
