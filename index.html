Sports Theater™

Sports Theater™ is the PHRINUSOMYIS™ concept for original competitive sporting experiences. It presents competition through a distinct sporting identity and a different approach to the traditional structure of sport.

It is a platform for original formats and competitions developed and administered by PHRINUSOMYIS™.
