Frequently Asked Questions.

What is PHRINUSOMYIS™?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organisation that develops, owns and administers original sporting competitions and sports properties.

Where is PHRINUSOMYIS™ headquartered?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is headquartered in Brooklyn, New York, United States.

What is ZEAVIQ SPORTS™?

ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ is the sporting division of PHRINUSOMYIS™ encompassing original competitive properties, sporting formats and organised live competitions.

What is VIKQUATHEL™?

VIKQUATHEL™ is a competitive sporting property within ZEAVIQ SPORTS™, developed and administered by PHRINUSOMYIS™.

Where can I find PHRINUSOMYIS™ news and publications?

Official news, announcements, statements, features and publications are available through PHRINUSOMYIS™ News.

Where can I access PHRINUSOMYIS™ Magazine?

PHRINUSOMYIS™ Magazine is available through the Magazine section.

How can I contact PHRINUSOMYIS™?
