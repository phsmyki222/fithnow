The Organization

A distinct identity in international sport.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization.

The organization develops, owns and administers sports competitions and Sports Theater™ worldwide. Its work includes the creation of original sporting formats that break away from traditional sports structures.

Through its competitions and sporting concepts, PHRINUSOMYIS™ establishes its own standards for competitive experiences, sporting presentation and international participation.

Original sporting formats. Distinct competitions. A different standard of competitive sport.

Sports Theater™

Beyond traditional sports structures.

Sports Theater™ is the sporting concept through which PHRINUSOMYIS™ presents original competitive formats and distinctive sporting experiences.

It brings together competition, performance, endurance, discipline and the human response to pressure within structured sporting environments.

Sports Theater™ is part of the organization's established sporting identity.

Original Sporting Formats

Competition beyond conventional boundaries.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ creates original sports formats with distinct competitive identities. Each format belongs to a wider sporting framework developed and administered by the organization.
