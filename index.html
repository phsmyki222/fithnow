The Organization

An International Sporting Organization. A Distinct Competitive Identity.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization that develops, owns and administers sports competitions and Sports Theater™ worldwide.

The organization creates original sporting formats that break away from traditional sports structures, with a distinct approach to competition, performance and the sporting experience.

Its activities encompass sporting competitions, intellectual property, event administration and the development of original sports concepts.

Organization Information

Sport is more than the contest. It is the standard of competition, the strength of performance and the character of the moment.

Sports Theater™

The Sporting World Beyond Traditional Structures.

Sports Theater™ is the PHRINUSOMYIS™ sporting concept through which original competitive formats are developed and presented.

It brings together competition, performance, endurance, discipline and the human response to pressure within distinctive sporting experiences.

Each competition carries its own identity while remaining part of the wider PHRINUSOMYIS™ sporting world.
