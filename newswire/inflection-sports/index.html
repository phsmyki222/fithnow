The Manifesto

Sport does not move evenly.

There are moments when everything changes. A second can decide a contest. A performance can redefine expectation. A record can establish a new standard. A result can redirect a season. A single moment can become part of sporting history.

INFLECTION SPORTS™ exists to identify these inflection points, report them and place them in context, following sport across disciplines, competitions and borders not simply to record what happened, but to understand what mattered, what changed and what comes next.

Because the final score tells us who won. The story tells us what changed.
