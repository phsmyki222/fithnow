COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

Reaching the Right Department

To help direct your correspondence appropriately, identify the subject of your enquiry clearly in your email. Include relevant organizational details, the purpose of your communication and any supporting information necessary for the department to understand your message.

Media representatives should use the Press & Media channel. Individuals and organizations contacting PHRINUSOMYIS™ about competitions and events should use Events & Competitions. Partnership proposals should be directed to Partnerships, while professional applications and employment enquiries should be sent to Careers & Employment.

For matters that do not correspond with these departments, the general correspondence address remains available. Please use the official communication channels displayed on this page when contacting PHRINUSOMYIS™.
