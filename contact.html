COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

Reaching the Right Department

To help direct your correspondence appropriately, identify the subject of your enquiry clearly in your email. Include relevant organizational details, the purpose of your communication and any supporting information necessary for the department to understand your message. Where your enquiry relates to an organization, institution or commercial entity, include the name of the organization and the capacity in which you are making contact.

Media representatives should use the Press & Media channel. Individuals and organizations contacting PHRINUSOMYIS™ about competitions and events should use Events & Competitions. Partnership proposals should be directed to Partnerships, while professional applications and employment enquiries should be sent to Careers & Employment.

For matters that do not correspond with these departments, the general correspondence address remains available. Selecting the appropriate communication channel helps keep enquiries organized and directs each message to the department responsible for that area. PHRINUSOMYIS™ welcomes clear, relevant and professionally presented correspondence through its official communication channels.
