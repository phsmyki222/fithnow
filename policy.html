PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how PHRINUSOMYIS™ handles personal information in connection with its official channels, communications, publications, events, competitions and authorized organizational activities.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization that develops, owns and administers sports competitions and ZEAVIQ SPORTS™ worldwide. The organization respects the privacy of individuals and handles personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. PURPOSE OF THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy describes the categories of personal information PHRINUSOMYIS™ may collect, the purposes for which information may be used, the circumstances in which it may be shared, and the measures applied to protect it.

It applies to personal information handled in connection with the organization's official digital channels, inquiries, communications, publications, events, competitions and other activities where the organization determines the purposes and means of processing.

Certain activities may be accompanied by additional privacy notices or specific terms. Where those notices apply, they should be read together with this Policy.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on the nature of an interaction or activity, PHRINUSOMYIS™ may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity information: name, title and other identifying details voluntarily supplied.
  • Contact information: email address, telephone number, business address and other contact details.
  • Professional information: organization, professional role, credentials and relevant experience where supplied for an organizational purpose.
  • Communication information: messages, inquiries, correspondence, proposals and documents submitted to the organization.
  • Event and competition information: registration, accreditation, participation and administrative details where relevant to an activity.
  • Media information: photographs, video, audio recordings and other media collected or supplied in connection with authorized activities.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, access logs, approximate location derived from technical data and platform usage information.
  • Preference information: language preferences and communication preferences where available.

The organization does not seek to collect personal information unrelated to the relevant purpose. The information collected depends on the individual's interaction with the organization.

3. HOW INFORMATION IS COLLECTED

Personal information may be obtained through:

  • Forms and submissions made through official organizational channels;
  • Emails, telephone calls and other direct communications;
  • Competition, event, accreditation or participation processes;
  • Professional, commercial or organizational correspondence;
  • Authorized photography, filming, recording and event documentation;
  • Cookies and similar technologies used by applicable digital services;
  • Service providers or other parties where collection is lawful and relevant to the stated purpose;
  • Publicly available professional or organizational sources, where appropriate and lawful.

4. HOW WE USE PERSONAL INFORMATION

PHRINUSOMYIS™ may use personal information for legitimate organizational and operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries and managing communications;
  • Administering organizational relationships and authorized activities;
  • Managing event registration, accreditation and participation;
  • Communicating official announcements and relevant information;
  • Preparing and distributing authorized publications and media materials;
  • Assessing proposals, applications and professional submissions;
  • Maintaining organizational, administrative and operational records;
  • Protecting the security and integrity of official digital channels and activities;
  • Detecting, investigating and addressing fraud, misuse or unauthorized conduct;
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations;
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal rights and claims.

Personal information will not be used for a materially different purpose without an appropriate lawful basis or other authorization required by applicable law.

5. LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, PHRINUSOMYIS™ processes personal information on one or more of the following grounds, as appropriate:

  • Consent: where an individual has provided valid consent for a specified purpose.
  • Contractual necessity: where processing is necessary to take steps at an individual's request or perform an applicable agreement.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required to comply with applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for legitimate organizational interests and those interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and interests.
  • Vital interests or public interest: where recognized by applicable law and relevant to the circumstances.

The applicable lawful basis depends on the particular information and the purpose for which it is processed.

6. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

PHRINUSOMYIS™ does not make personal information available to unrelated parties simply because it has been collected. Information may be disclosed where necessary and lawful, including to:

  • Authorized personnel and representatives who require the information for their responsibilities;
  • Service providers supporting relevant organizational operations, technology, communications or administration;
  • Event, competition or operational partners where disclosure is necessary and appropriately authorized;
  • Professional advisers, auditors and insurers where relevant;
  • Public authorities, regulators, courts or law enforcement agencies where legally required or permitted;
  • Other parties where the individual has provided valid consent or disclosure is otherwise lawful.

Where appropriate, service providers are required to handle personal information under contractual confidentiality, security and data protection obligations.

The organization does not sell personal information in exchange for payment. Any sharing for advertising, analytics or other purposes will be subject to applicable law and the relevant privacy choices or consent requirements.

7. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization. Depending on the activity, personal information may be accessed or processed in countries other than the country in which it was collected.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, the organization will apply appropriate safeguards and transfer mechanisms where required by applicable data protection law.

The safeguards used may include contractual protections, recognized adequacy arrangements or other lawful transfer mechanisms applicable to the relevant jurisdictions.

8. DATA RETENTION

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including the administration of organizational activities, the maintenance of required records and compliance with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary according to the nature of the information, the relationship involved, applicable legal requirements and the need to establish or defend legal claims.

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted, anonymized or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements and the organization's retention practices.

9. DATA SECURITY

PHRINUSOMYIS™ applies reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

Depending on the nature of the information and the relevant processing activity, safeguards may include access controls, restricted permissions, secure communications, administrative procedures and appropriate service-provider requirements.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. The organization therefore cannot guarantee absolute security, but takes reasonable steps to protect information within its control.

10. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Official digital channels may use cookies and similar technologies to support functionality, understand usage, maintain preferences and improve the user experience.

Cookies may include:

  • Essential cookies: technologies necessary for core functions.
  • Preference cookies: technologies that remember settings such as language preferences.
  • Analytics cookies: technologies used, where enabled, to understand how digital services are used.
  • Third-party cookies: technologies placed or managed by external services integrated into an organizational channel.

Where required, consent will be requested before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used. Users may also be able to manage cookies through their browser settings.

Additional information is provided in the organization's Cookie Policy.

11. MEDIA, PHOTOGRAPHY AND EVENT RECORDINGS

Sports competitions, events and other authorized organizational activities may involve photography, filming, audio recording or other media documentation.

Where individuals are identifiable in media materials, the organization will handle such materials in accordance with applicable privacy, image-rights, publicity and data protection requirements.

Event-specific notices, participation terms, accreditation conditions or consent arrangements may provide further information about recording and publication.

Individuals should consult the applicable event notice or contact the organization where they have questions about the use of identifiable media.

12. CHILDREN AND MINORS

Where an activity involves children or minors, PHRINUSOMYIS™ will apply the consent, authorization, safeguarding and data protection requirements imposed by applicable law.

Where required, consent or authorization will be obtained from a parent, legal guardian or other person legally authorized to provide it.

The organization does not knowingly seek personal information from children through general communication channels where such collection is not appropriate or lawfully authorized.

13. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on the applicable data protection law and the circumstances of processing, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to personal information;
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • The right to request deletion of information in circumstances provided by law;
  • The right to request restriction of certain processing;
  • The right to object to certain processing;
  • The right to request transfer of information where data portability applies;
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal conditions, exceptions and limitations.

To exercise a relevant right, an individual may contact PHRINUSOMYIS™ through its official Contact page. The organization may request information reasonably necessary to verify identity and assess the request.

14. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND EXTERNAL CHANNELS

Official organizational channels may contain links to third-party digital services, social media platforms, applications or other external resources.

Those external services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. PHRINUSOMYIS™ does not control independent third-party privacy practices and encourages individuals to review the applicable notices before providing personal information.

15. AUTOMATED PROCESSING

Certain digital services used in connection with official organizational channels may process technical or usage information automatically for security, functionality, analytics or administrative purposes.

Where applicable law grants rights relating to automated decision-making or profiling, individuals may exercise those rights in accordance with the relevant legal requirements.

This Policy does not represent that PHRINUSOMYIS™ uses automated decision-making to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects unless separately disclosed in an applicable notice.

16. DATA BREACHES

If a personal data breach occurs, PHRINUSOMYIS™ will assess the incident and take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

Where notification is legally required, the organization will notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected individuals within the applicable legal timeframes and according to the relevant requirements.

17. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

PHRINUSOMYIS™ may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in its organizational activities, information practices, technologies or applicable legal requirements.

The revised Policy will be made available through the relevant official channel, together with an updated effective or revision date.

Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent, the organization will follow those requirements.

18. CONTACT AND PRIVACY REQUESTS

Questions, privacy requests and communications concerning this Policy may be directed to PHRINUSOMYIS™ through its official contact channels.

Visit the official Contact page to submit a privacy-related inquiry or request.

19. RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER POLICIES

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the organization's applicable Terms of Use, Cookie Policy and any additional privacy notices issued for specific events, competitions, applications or organizational activities.

Where a specific notice provides additional information about a particular processing activity, that notice applies to the activity it describes, subject to applicable law.
