01 / Privacy framework

Respect for information. Responsibility in practice.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ is an international sports organization headquartered in New York, USA, with operations across 21 countries. Its activities include the development, ownership and administration of sports competitions and original sports formats, together with its official communications, editorial publications and related services.

This Privacy Policy sets out the principles governing the collection, use, storage, disclosure and protection of personal information in connection with PHRINUSOMYIS™. It applies to interactions with the organization's official website and other services where this policy is presented.

The policy should be read together with any specific privacy notice, registration terms or event conditions presented when a particular service is used.

02

Information We Handle

The information handled by PHRINUSOMYIS™ depends on the nature of an individual's interaction with the organization. It may include:

  • Identity information: name, title and other identifying details supplied directly by an individual.
  • Contact information: email address, telephone number, business address and correspondence details.
  • Professional information: organization, role, professional background and relevant qualifications supplied in connection with a professional enquiry or engagement.
  • Participation information: details reasonably required for competition administration, event registration, accreditation or related sporting activities.
  • Communication records: messages, enquiries, requests and other correspondence directed to the organization.
  • Technical information: device type, browser, operating system, general access records and information generated through use of digital services.
  • Media information: photographs, video, audio or other materials created or supplied in connection with an authorized event, publication or organizational activity.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ does not require individuals to provide information that is unrelated to the purpose of their interaction. Where particular information is necessary for a service, event or formal engagement, that requirement will be communicated in the relevant context.

03

How Information Is Collected

Information may be obtained through direct interaction with PHRINUSOMYIS™, including when an individual:

  • Submits an enquiry or sends correspondence.
  • Uses an official website or digital service.
  • Registers interest in or participates in an authorized competition or event.
  • Applies for a professional, organizational or representative engagement.
  • Submits material for editorial, media or publication consideration.
  • Communicates with an authorized representative of the organization.

Certain technical information may also be generated automatically when a website or digital service is accessed. Information may, where appropriate and lawful, be received from authorized service providers or other parties involved in a specific organizational activity.

04

Use of Information

Personal information is handled for purposes connected with the organization's legitimate operations and the services or activities with which an individual engages. These purposes may include:

  • Responding to enquiries and managing correspondence.
  • Administering organizational, professional and commercial relationships.
  • Managing competition participation, event operations and related administration.
  • Maintaining the operation, reliability and security of digital services.
  • Producing and administering authorized editorial, media and publication activities.
  • Communicating relevant organizational information.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory and contractual responsibilities.
  • Protecting the rights, property, personnel and legitimate interests of PHRINUSOMYIS™ and other parties.
  • Reviewing and improving the effectiveness and performance of organizational services.

Information is not used for purposes materially unrelated to the reason for its collection without an appropriate legal basis or, where required, the individual's consent.

05

Legal Grounds for Processing

Where applicable privacy legislation requires a legal basis for processing personal information, PHRINUSOMYIS™ relies on the basis appropriate to the particular activity. These may include:

  • Consent: where an individual has given permission for a defined purpose.
  • Contractual necessity: where processing is required to enter into or perform an agreement.
  • Legal obligations: where information must be handled to comply with applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is reasonably necessary for legitimate organizational purposes and does not override applicable individual rights.
  • Vital interests or public interest: where recognized under applicable law and relevant to the circumstances.

The applicable legal basis may differ according to the jurisdiction, the service involved and the circumstances of the processing.

06

Information Sharing and Disclosure

PHRINUSOMYIS™ does not treat personal information as unrestricted public information. Disclosure is limited to circumstances connected with legitimate organizational purposes, applicable legal requirements or the individual's instructions.

Information may be shared where appropriate with:

  • Authorized personnel and representatives responsible for the relevant activity.
  • Service providers supporting website operations, communications, hosting, security or administration.
  • Competition organizers, event partners or authorized operational participants where information is necessary for an activity.
  • Professional advisers, auditors or other appointed specialists where their involvement is necessary.
  • Public authorities, courts or regulatory bodies where disclosure is required or permitted by law.
  • Other parties where an individual has authorized the disclosure or where another lawful basis applies.

Where external service providers process information on behalf of PHRINUSOMYIS™, appropriate contractual or other safeguards may be used to govern that processing.

PHRINUSOMYIS™ does not state that every service provider or recipient is located in the same country. International handling is addressed in the section below.

07

International Information Transfers

PHRINUSOMYIS™ operates across 21 countries, with its headquarters in New York, USA. Depending on the nature of an organizational activity and the service providers involved, information may be accessed or processed in more than one jurisdiction.

Where personal information is transferred across national borders, PHRINUSOMYIS™ takes steps appropriate to the circumstances and applicable law to protect that information. Such steps may include contractual safeguards, recognized transfer mechanisms or other protections required by the relevant jurisdiction.

International access does not remove the obligation to handle personal information responsibly or in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

08

Information Retention

Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, manage an ongoing relationship, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes or meet legal and regulatory obligations.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the nature of the engagement, applicable legal requirements and legitimate organizational needs.

When information is no longer required, it may be securely deleted, anonymized or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable retention and record-management practices.

09

Information Security

PHRINUSOMYIS™ applies reasonable organizational, administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

The nature of the safeguards depends on the information involved, the systems used and the risks associated with the relevant activity. Access to information should be limited to individuals and service providers with a legitimate operational requirement.

No website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Individuals should use appropriate care when transmitting information through digital channels.

10

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The PHRINUSOMYIS™ website and related digital services may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, user preferences, performance measurement or other disclosed purposes.

Cookies are small data files that may be stored by a browser when a website is visited. Depending on their purpose, they may help a website remember settings, maintain essential functions or understand how visitors interact with its pages.

Where consent is required by applicable law, non-essential cookies or similar technologies should only be used in accordance with the relevant consent requirements.

Browser settings may allow individuals to review, restrict or remove cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or operation of some website functions.

The specific technologies in use may vary as the website and its supporting services change. Any additional disclosures required by applicable law will be provided in the relevant context.

11

Website and Digital Services

When an individual accesses the official PHRINUSOMYIS™ website or another digital service, certain technical information may be processed to support the operation and security of that service.

This may include:

  • Internet protocol address and general connection information.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages or resources requested.
  • Dates, times and technical records of access.
  • Information relevant to diagnosing errors, preventing misuse and maintaining service performance.

Technical records may be generated by hosting providers, security services, analytics services or other infrastructure supporting the relevant digital service. Their handling is also subject to applicable provider terms and privacy requirements.

12

Publications, Editorial and Media Activities

PHRINUSOMYIS™ produces and administers official communications, editorial material and publications, including PHRINUSOMYIS™ Magazine and its official Newswire channel.

In connection with these activities, names, images, statements, interviews, photographs, video recordings or other materials may be processed or published where there is an appropriate legal basis, authorization or other lawful justification.

Editorial and media materials are handled according to the purpose of the relevant publication or activity and applicable legal requirements. Where an individual submits material for consideration, submission does not automatically guarantee publication.

Requests concerning published personal information may be considered in light of applicable privacy rights, editorial responsibilities, recordkeeping obligations and other relevant legal considerations.

13

Sports Competitions and Participation

Personal information may be required to administer sports competitions, sporting activities, event participation, accreditation and related organizational operations.

Depending on the activity, this may include participant identity details, registration information, professional or sporting credentials, team or representative details, event records, photographs and authorized recordings.

Where a particular competition or event requires additional information or involves separate participation conditions, the relevant notice or documentation will apply alongside this policy.

Information relating to a participant will be handled for legitimate sporting, administrative, safety, communication and recordkeeping purposes, subject to applicable law.

14

Children's Privacy

PHRINUSOMYIS™ recognizes the importance of protecting children's personal information. Where an activity involves a child, information must be handled in accordance with the age requirements, consent rules and other protections established by applicable law.

Where parental or guardian authorization is legally required, the relevant authorization must be obtained before the applicable information is processed.

Any individual who believes that a child's personal information has been provided or handled improperly may contact PHRINUSOMYIS™ through its official contact channel.

15

Individual Privacy Rights

Depending on the applicable jurisdiction and the circumstances of the processing, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether personal information is being processed.
  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable law provides that right.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to particular processing where a legal right to object applies.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with a competent privacy or data protection authority.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exceptions, recordkeeping requirements, the rights of other individuals and other applicable limitations.

Requests may be subject to reasonable identity verification to protect personal information from unauthorized disclosure. PHRINUSOMYIS™ will consider and respond to eligible requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

16

Third-Party Service Providers

Certain functions associated with the website, communications, digital distribution, security, hosting and organizational administration may depend on services provided by external organizations.

These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services. Their handling of information may be governed by their own privacy policies, contractual obligations and applicable laws.

The presence of an external service on or in connection with a PHRINUSOMYIS™ channel does not, by itself, mean that PHRINUSOMYIS™ controls all of that provider's systems or independent data practices.

17

External Websites and Links

Official PHRINUSOMYIS™ channels may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. These links may be provided for reference, information or access to an external service.

External websites operate under their own privacy practices and terms. PHRINUSOMYIS™ does not control the privacy policies or information-handling practices of independent third-party websites.

Individuals should review the applicable privacy information of an external service before providing personal information to that service.

18

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised to reflect changes in applicable law, organizational operations, digital services, information-handling practices or other relevant circumstances.

The current version published on the official PHRINUSOMYIS™ website is the applicable published version. Where a change requires specific notice or consent under applicable law, the relevant requirements will be followed.

Individuals are encouraged to consult this page periodically to remain informed about the organization's privacy framework.

19

Privacy Enquiries and Official Contact

Questions, requests or concerns relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information may be directed to PHRINUSOMYIS™ through its official contact channel.

A privacy request should identify the nature of the enquiry and provide sufficient information for the organization to understand and assess it. Sensitive personal information should not be included unless it is necessary for the request.

Privacy-related requests are assessed according to their subject matter, the information involved and the legal requirements applicable to the individual and the organization.

Official correspondence

Privacy matters are handled with care.

For privacy enquiries, information requests or matters concerning the handling of personal information, use the official PHRINUSOMYIS™ contact channel.
