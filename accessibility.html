01 Organizational Standard

Accessibility Commitment

PHRINUSOMYIS™ recognizes accessibility as an important part of how the organization communicates information and presents its activities to audiences with different communication and viewing needs.

Accessibility is considered in the presentation of organizational information, official publications and audiovisual material. This includes attention to readable text, clear navigation, caption availability and compatibility with commonly used viewing devices.

The availability of a particular accessibility feature may depend on the content, media format, device or external platform through which it is accessed.

02 Audiovisual Information

Closed Captions & Subtitles

Captions and subtitles provide a text-based representation of spoken content in audiovisual material. Depending on the format, captions may also identify speakers and describe relevant non-speech sounds, including music and sound effects.

Closed Captions

Text that can be switched on or off when the media player or platform supports caption controls.

Subtitles

On-screen text presenting spoken dialogue, including translated dialogue where a translation is available.

Descriptive Captions

Captions that can include relevant sound effects, music cues and speaker identification in addition to dialogue.

Transcripts

Written versions of spoken content that may be provided where suitable for the particular audio or video material.

Media Availability

Caption and subtitle availability varies by individual media item. Where caption files or subtitle tracks are provided, viewers can use the controls available in the relevant media player.

03 International Communication

Language Access

PHRINUSOMYIS™ communicates with audiences across different language communities. Language access may be supported through translated organizational information, subtitles or other language resources where available.

The language selector provides machine-assisted translation of displayed information through Google Translate. This function is separate from video captions and does not automatically translate the audio or subtitle tracks of every video.

The languages available in a particular video depend on the subtitle or caption tracks supplied for that media. Viewers should check the media player's language settings where those controls are provided.

04 Media Controls

Viewing & Display Options

Where supported by the media player or viewing platform, users may be able to adjust caption settings to suit their preferences.

  • Turn captions or subtitles on or off.
  • Select an available subtitle language.
  • Adjust caption text size or appearance where supported.
  • Use device-level accessibility and display settings.
  • Access transcripts where they are provided.

These controls are provided by the relevant player, browser or device. Their availability may differ across platforms.

05 Digital Information

Accessibility of Organizational Information

PHRINUSOMYIS™ considers the accessibility of its published information through clear content structure, readable text, descriptive image text, accessible navigation and consideration of different screen sizes.

Some features or third-party services may not provide the same experience across every browser, assistive technology or device. The organization welcomes reports identifying barriers that prevent audiences from accessing information or using supported features.

If you encounter a difficulty, contact the accessibility address below and identify the relevant page, feature or media. Where possible, include the device and browser being used so the issue can be reviewed.

06 Audience Correspondence

Accessibility Requests & Feedback

Audiences may contact PHRINUSOMYIS™ to report an accessibility issue, request information about available captions or subtitles, or provide feedback about the accessibility of an organizational information or media experience.

When submitting a request, include the relevant page address or media title and a brief description of the difficulty encountered. Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the request.

Accessibility Support

Accessibility enquiries can be sent to the organization's designated contact address. Requests will be reviewed and addressed where reasonably possible.
