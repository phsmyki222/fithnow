A world in motion

Some moments pass. Others define their time.

The world is always becoming. Power changes hands. Ideas cross borders. Records fall. Fortunes are made. Cultures evolve. New voices emerge, and familiar places are seen anew.

DAXYREN is an international monthly devoted to the people, events, ideas and achievements shaping our time. Its interest is not in everything that happens. It is in what matters.

Across world affairs, business, sport, culture, innovation and contemporary life, DAXYREN brings the consequential, remarkable and enduring into focus.

The editorial perspective

Beyond the immediate headline lies the context, consequence and human significance of the story.

Not simply the story of the moment, but the meaning within it.

There is a difference between what happens and what deserves to be remembered. DAXYREN follows that distinction.

It follows the currents moving through societies, economies, institutions and everyday life. It recognises achievement, examines change and gives attention to the ideas and events that help define an era.

Its pages bring together different subjects and perspectives without losing sight of the wider world they belong to. Every issue is a considered record of a world in motion.

The present does not wait to become history. It is becoming history now.
